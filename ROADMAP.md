# Roadmap (proposal)

The public roadmap lives on GitHub, so anyone can see what's planned and weigh in.

- **Ideas and questions** start in [Discussions](https://github.com/nan-labs/fleeet/discussions) under *Ideas*. Upvotes there help with prioritising.
- **Accepted work** becomes an issue with the `roadmap` label, linked back to its discussion.
- **Milestones** group issues by release. The [milestones page](https://github.com/nan-labs/fleeet/milestones?state=all) is the roadmap view.
- **Status labels:** `roadmap` (accepted), `help wanted` (open for contributors), `needs proposal` (needs a write-up first).

The roadmap covers the open kit and the parts of the hosted board that the kit depends on. Hosted-only features may be listed, but their timing is the maintainer's call. Nothing on the roadmap is a commitment to a date.
